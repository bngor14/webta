Network
01

Securing a Retail Chain's Network Infrastructure

85%Less downtime
0Breach incidents
40%Faster speeds
The Client

A mid-sized retail company with 12 branches across Vietnam, managing point-of-sale systems and customer payment data across an unsegmented legacy network.

The Problem

The client experienced frequent network outages and suffered a data breach that exposed customer payment records. Their existing infrastructure lacked network segmentation, had no intrusion detection, and used outdated firewall rules that were easy to bypass.

Our Solution

Townyd redesigned the entire network architecture from the ground up: implemented VLANs to isolate critical POS systems from general traffic, deployed a next-generation firewall with custom rule sets, installed a real-time IDS, and established an encrypted VPN for all inter-branch communication. Full documentation and staff security training were included in the handover.

The Result

Network downtime reduced by 85% within the first month. Zero security incidents recorded over the following 6 months. Data transfer speeds improved by 40% due to optimized routing protocols. The client renewed with Townyd for ongoing monitoring and maintenance.

Software + Data
02

Sales Analytics Platform for an E-Commerce Startup

60%Fewer errors
2hrsReport generation
LiveDashboard data
The Client

A growing Vietnamese e-commerce startup processing over 10,000 monthly orders across three product categories, using manual spreadsheets across multiple departments.

The Problem

The client was managing all sales and inventory data across disconnected spreadsheets with no unified system. This led to reporting errors, delayed business decisions, and an inability to track real-time performance across product lines or sales channels.

Our Solution

We developed a custom web-based analytics dashboard using React and Node.js, backed by a normalized MySQL database. The platform integrated with their existing payment gateway via REST API, supported multi-user access with role-based permissions, and featured automated daily report delivery via email. Deployment was on AWS EC2 with an RDS backend for scalability.

The Result

Reporting time dropped from 3 business days to under 2 hours. Order processing errors decreased by 60%. Management gained real-time visibility into sales data across all categories. The client scaled their team from 5 to 18 members within 6 months of the launch, citing the platform as a critical factor.
